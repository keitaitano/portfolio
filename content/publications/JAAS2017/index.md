abstract: "Laser ablation ICP-MS is a widely used technique for measuring trace elements and isotopes in minerals, but unwanted oxide molecules formed during analysis can interfere with measurements. In this study, we systematically investigated how rare earth elements, thorium, and uranium form oxides across various minerals and glasses. Our findings clarify the physical mechanisms of oxide production and provide practical insights for improving analytical accuracy in geochemical microanalysis."

### Key Highlights
- **Systematic Oxide Analysis:** Comprehensively quantifies oxide production rates for rare earth elements (REEs), Th, and U across synthetic and natural phosphate minerals, glasses, and zircon.
- **Mechanism of Interference:** Uncovers how oxide formation behaviors vary depending on bond dissociation energies and plasma gas flow rates, providing clear guidelines to minimize spectral interference in microanalysis.
