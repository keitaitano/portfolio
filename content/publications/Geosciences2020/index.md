abstract: "Monazite is a valuable mineral for dating geological events and tracking the origins of sediments, but it can form in both igneous (granitic) and metamorphic rocks, making its source difficult to pinpoint. In this study, we applied multinomial logistic regression, a machine-learning classification method, to monazite trace-element data. The model successfully distinguished monazite source rocks with high accuracy (~97%), demonstrating how data-driven geochemistry can clarify sediment provenance and past Earth history."

### Key Highlights
- **AI-Driven Source Classification:** Uses multinomial logistic regression and cross-validation to distinguish whether monazite originated from granitic or metamorphic rocks with ~97% accuracy.
- **Key Trace Element Markers:** Identifies critical chemical discriminators (including light REEs, Eu, and Th) that reflect elemental behavior during magmatic and metamorphic processes.
- **Sediment Provenance Application:** Applies the classification model to river sands to trace sediment sources, showcasing practical applications for reconstructing geological history.
