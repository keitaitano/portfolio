Overview: "The two zircon U–Pb age ranges of a hornblende peridotite from the Hida Belt reveal that the mafic magma reservoir can be sustained for ~10^7 yr, consistent with the time scale of felsic reservoirs. The contrasting geochemical characteristics between the older and younger zircons suggest that mantle-derived mafic magmas played a key role in maintaining the long-lived reservoir and continental crust evolution."

### Key Highlights
- **Magma Reservoir Longevity:** Zircon age ranges demonstrate that mafic magma reservoirs can persist for up to ~10⁷ years.
- **Crustal Evolution:** Highlights the role of mantle-derived mafic magmas in sustaining magma systems and shaping island arc crustal evolution.
