### Key Highlights
- **Corrosion & Redox Management:** Reviews how harsh molten salts degrade candidate structural alloys and discusses chemical and electrochemical approaches to control and prevent corrosion.
- **Data-Driven & In Situ Diagnostics:** Discusses the application of data-driven/machine-learning approaches and advanced *in situ* electrochemical measurements for chemical-state identification and materials compatibility assessment.
