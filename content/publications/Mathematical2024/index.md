abstract: "Zircon is an extremely durable mineral that records the history of ancient rocks through its trace-element 'fingerprints'. Determining which rock type a zircon grain originally crystallized in is vital for reconstructing Earth's geological past, but conventional geochemical discrimination diagrams often suffer from overlapping signatures. In this study, we employed modern machine-learning classification models on compiled zircon trace-element datasets to systematically classify zircon source rocks, identifying key chemical indicators (such as P and Sc) and significantly improving source-rock discrimination accuracy."

### Key Highlights
- **Machine-Learning Provenance Classification:** Develops and evaluates machine-learning models to reliably classify the source-rock lithologies of zircon grains based on multidimensional trace-element fingerprints.
- **Key Diagnostic Elements:** Demonstrates the high diagnostic importance of previously underutilized elements (such as P and Sc) alongside rare earth elements to overcome overlap in traditional binary/ternary discrimination diagrams.
- **Improved Source Identification:** Provides a modern, objective data-driven framework for sediment provenance studies and tracing continental crust evolution.
